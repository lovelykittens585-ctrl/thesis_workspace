SOCIAL MEDIA IS STIFLING. SO MUCH INFO ALL AT ONCE AND MY DUMBASS WANTS TO BE A PART OF THE CONVERSATION(S) BECAUSE I LOVE A PUBLIC FORUM.
(ALTHOUGH, THEY ARE NOT ALWAYS EQUAL, AT LEAST ON A BULLETIN BOARD EVERYTHING EXISTS AT ONCE IN ONE VISIBLE SPACE).
IT’S TOO MUCH TO THINK ABOUT, TOO MUCH TO TAKE TO HEART, LOTS OF MORAL POLICING, SO MUCH SO YOU BEGIN TO QUESTION YOURSELF. SO MUCH SO, YOU GET LOST IN ALL THE INFO AND GET CAUGHT IN DISTRIBUTING THE SAME INFO.

I ENJOY THAT THE INTERNET IS A PUBLIC FORUM, BUT IF NELSON HAD HIS WAY

EVERY REFERENCE,
EVERY NOTE
AND COMMENT

WOULD EXIST ALL AT ONCE AND PROBABLY AT THE SAME TIME. LESS AN INFINITE SCROLL AND MORE OF A BIG OLE' BULLETIN BOARD THAT LINKS BACK TO ONE ANOTHER IN REAL TIME (NOT PAGE AFTER PAGE).
I'M STILL FASCINATED BY THE CONCEPT
(OF HIS ORIGINAL IDEA FOR HYPERLINKS, THAT IS, NOT BULLETIN BOARDS, BUT MAYBE THAT TOO)

IF THE INTERNET DOESN’T MAKE YOU QUESTION YOUR EXISTENCE IS IT REALLY THE INTERNET LOL?
ALL THIS TO SAY, I HAD A REALLY GOOD CONVERSATION ABOUT THIRD SPACES RECENTLY.
ONE THAT MADE ME QUESTION WHAT THE WORD ACTUALLY MEANT IN AN ERA WHERE EVERY WORD IS A BUZZWORD.

THE INTERNET IS A THIRD SPACE BECAUSE IT IS DISTINCT FROM HOME AND WORK.
IT ALSO COMBINES THE TWO BUT IT IS NOT RECOGNIZABLE AS JUST HOME, OR WORK.

PEOPLE FORM COMMUNITIES WITHIN THE SPACE, SOME MORE GATEKEPT THAN OTHERS, SOME MORE PUBLIC, SOME MORE PRIVATE.
IT’S A CONTESTED SPACE, SIMILAR TO THAT OF A LIBRARY OR ANY PLACE THAT DISSEMINATES INFO.

A THIRD SPACE ISN’T NECESSARILY A SENTIMENTAL, PERSONAL THING IT IS A SPACE THAT IS NEITHER HOME NOR WORK.
WE SHOULD TREAT THE INTERNET AS SUCH. SOME PEOPLE LIVE ON THE INTERNET, SOME PEOPLE WORK ON THE INTERNET.
IF IT WAS ONE OR THE OTHER IT WOULDN’T BE A THIRD SPACE;
IT WOULD NOT BE THE INTERNET.
AGAIN, THE INTERNET USED TO BE MY THIRD SPACE BUT NOWADAYS NOT SO MUCH.
IT’S A SIMILAR THING TO GOING TO YOUR FAVORITE COFFEE SHOP AND OUTGROWING IT.
NOT TO SAY I’VE OUTGROWN THE INTERNET (CLEARLY I HAVEN’T)
BUT I’M ACUTELY AWARE OF MY PLACEMENT WITHIN IT. I'M HAPPY TO STAY THERE.
A RECURRING CUSTOMER YES, WILLING TO GIVE MY ENTIRE IDENTITY TO IT NO.

THE PURPOSE OF THIS NEXT SECTION IS TO EXAMINE WHY WE SHOULD BE THINKING ABOUT OUR LIVES IN CYBERSPACE ALONGSIDE OUR LIVES AWAY FROM THE KEYBOARD. THEY’RE TWO SIDES OF THE SAME COIN.
I BELIEVE WE NEED BOTH IN ORDER TO PROCESS THE WORLD AROUND US.
WE’RE TOO CONNECTED WITH CYBERSPACE TO EXCLUDE IT AND YET,
WE SHOULDN’T SURRENDER TO IT IN ORDER TO ESCAPE OUR LIVES AWAY FROM THE KEYBOARD.
