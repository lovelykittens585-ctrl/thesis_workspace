Defining Terms

Some defining terms/concepts that I’d need to work out in order to due this would be:

  • Place vs Space
  • Hyperreal/Hyperreality
  • Reality/Alternative Reality
  • The difference between simulacrum and simulation
  • Netizen
I am aware that my scope is primarily informed by my own experiences on the internet, while this is the groundwork I’m working with, I hope to expand my scope by utilizing field notes (i.e. interviews, personal journals, drawings) throughout the thesis process.

There are questions of belonging and who gets to be what on the internet, how we see each other(i.e. celebrity, fandom and politics) and what the Internet was really made for.

In addition to this, I’d like to look at how personal identity is crafted through the internet (i.e. social media) and how these identities bridge the gap between reality and simulation; Utilizing Baudrillard’s concepts of simulacrum and hyperreality. I would also like to analyze music, video/tv and literature that play with these concepts and give us glimpses into what is/could be as an addtional source of insight. This is a list of readings/texts I plan to utilize during my time in Thesis:
  • Digitizing Race by Lisa Nakamura
  • The Celebrity Persona Pandemic by P. David Marshall
  • Reading Simulacra: Fatal Theories for Postmodernity by M.W Smith
  • Glitch Feminism by Legacy Russel
  • Intimacy and the Machine: Slouching Towards Girlblogging by Biz Sherbert
  • Otaku: Japan's Database Animals by Hiroki Azuma
  • Simulacra and Simulation by Jean Baudrillard
