Abstract

My thesis concerns the Internet as reality/alternative reality. I want to find out how we operate and exist on the internet.

In order to explore this question through engaging with various texts (i.e. Azuma, Baudrillard, Russell, etc), media (i.e. music, video, gaming) and embodied/personal experience. I hope to discover why we often seek out the Internet as a secondary source for interfacing with the world around us, as I find it important to unpack our relationships with it rather than reject it. If we work to understand and unpack, we can better utilize the internet to navigate new modes of being and survival.

Research Questions

My research questions are formatted into 3 categories:

    1.Brief history of the Internet

  • When was the Internet created? (i.e. Web 1.0-Web 3.0)
  • Who and what has access to the Internet?
  • Who/What was the the internet made for?

    • 2. The Internet as a tool for identity and self-expression

  • How has social media changed the way people exist on the internet?
  • How has the internet changed the way we look at people? (e.g. memes, trends, celebrity)
  • Can the internet be used as a tool to exist outside of ourselves?

    • 3. The Internet as a simulation of reality

  • How does our reality blend with the Internet?
  • How are they separate from one another?
