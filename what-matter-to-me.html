What matters to me right now is how we operate and interface on the internet.

Right now, I’m most likely working on a laptop, while music from my Ipad plays in the background. I have a blog that I utilize to write and talk about anything I want. I have an Instagram account (that I avoid) that I post my art on.

So far, what I’ve listed has everything to do with the physical technologies we interface with before we go online. I’d argue that we are always online. The second we connect to Wi-Fi, the second we can enter a search term in Google or browse an online shopping catalog. We are constantly being bombarded with the Internet in our daily lives; As soon as we could take our phones with us on the go, the internet became not just a space you go to and leave but a place we could reside and operate in. This is fascinating to me, because I grew up with the Internet as a place. I think the first personal electronic I was gifted was a Kindle Fire, I didn’t end up getting a phone until 6th grade. On this Kindle Fire, I would discover sites like Wattpad, Ao3, Tumblr and Instagram. I soon realized that I could do and be whoever I wanted on the internet. As salacious as those sites could be at times, I discovered communities that set the groundwork for ideologies, interests and identities.

Previously, fandom was of interest to me, because a lot of my time on the internet was spent interacting with online communities. A large part of why I’m an artist today is because I wanted to make fanart and write fanfiction to further contribute and engage in communities I loved. I’ve made friends who I’ve known for years because of fandom both online and offline. So I hope to utilize some of this previous research to ground parts of my current research.

I am primarily interested in how the internet exists as reality and/or alternative reality. The internet is far too large to disconnect it from our own reality (or reality as we know it through the five senses), it started here. What I really want to know is how when we operate and exist on the internet, could this be utilized for something greater than social media clout? How has connecting with one another changed? Who have we become due to this place that we call the Internet?
